The native source is daily Intervals.icu Wellness `carbohydrates`, `protein` and `fatTotal`, all in grams. These already reach the client as raw `wellness_history`; workout energy expenditure, body weight and intake-calorie fields are not substitutes. The official source-contract investigation and primary links are in NATIVE-PEAK-CAPABILITIES.md. No additional nutrition request is needed when the selected history range is loaded.

Implemented source helpers aggregate recorded daily grams into athlete-calendar day/Monday-week/month buckets. Display calories use general4/4/9 conversion for carbohydrates/protein/fat; percentages use each macro's energy divided by their combined recorded energy. The general factors are supported by the [FDA's food-science guide](https://www.fda.gov/media/95613/download). This does not establish TrainingPeaks' unpublished nutrition conversion, averaging or rounding rules.

Missing days stay missing. Explicit numeric zero remains a recorded zero; a zero-energy percentage stays unavailable. Unknown nutrients never become zero. Exact duplicate daily records do not double totals; conflicting values for the same date remain unavailable, independent of input order. A period nutrient total is available only when that nutrient is known on every recorded nutrition day in the period. Percentages require all three period nutrients. Tooltips expose the actual date range, recorded grams/energy and recorded-day coverage; partial/conflicting coverage is identified. Invalid values/dates, conflicting source identities and records outside the selected window cannot invent nutrition. Bounded input/range errors are explicit instead of truncating results.
