AR Performance privacy policy

Updated October 6, 2026

AR Performance is a personal training and nutrition application operated by Alex Russell. This page describes how the application uses information from your connected accounts and information you enter.

Information used

When you connect Intervals.icu, the application reads your athlete profile, planned and completed workouts, workout recordings including GPS routes and performance measurements, training settings, and wellness measurements. It also stores nutrition entries, training plans, comments, and preferences you enter. Account credentials and authorization tokens allow the backend to access the services you connect.

Purpose and storage

This information powers your calendar, workout maps and charts, nutrition diary, training analysis, and coaching features. The application caches information on your device and stores settings, recent training data, nutrition entries, and prepared workout views in Supabase. Historical recordings may be retrieved from Intervals.icu when you open them. Training data and generated reports can also be stored in your configured GitHub repository.

Backend connection credentials are stored in the deployment environment or encrypted settings. Access to personal application pages requires authentication. Device caches are intended to make repeat visits faster; use a trusted device when viewing your training data.

Service providers

Vercel hosts the application, Supabase provides backend storage, Intervals.icu provides training data, GitHub provides the configured training repository, and FatSecret provides food search and nutrition information. Coaching features send the information needed to answer your request or generate a report to OpenAI. These providers process information according to their own policies. The application does not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can revoke the application's authorization in your Intervals.icu settings. Revoking authorization stops access through that authorization and its webhook delivery; it does not automatically erase existing device caches, database records, or GitHub exports. If you also supplied a personal API key, remove or revoke that key to stop that separate connection.

To request removal of stored information or ask about this policy, contact Alex Russell, the application owner, through the channel through which you received access to AR Performance. Removal requests must cover any configured GitHub exports as well as application storage. You can clear local browser data through your browser settings.
